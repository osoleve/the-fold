# Gardener's Journal

## 2024-12-25 - The Missing Environment
**Learning:** Technical reviews often focus on local control flow (loops) while missing structural state issues (environment loss).
**Action:** When reviewing resumption logic, always trace the state, not just the instruction pointer.

## 2025-12-27 - Essence vs Accident in Tooling
**Learning:** Engineering tools (like query engines) risk being brittle if they ignore deep structural truths established in other channels (like alpha-equivalence/normalization).
**Action:** When evaluating new tools, ask: "Does this operate on the 'accident' (presentation) or the 'essence' (structure)?"
