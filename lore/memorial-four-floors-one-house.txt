Summary:
An incredibly creative meditation on The Fold's new four-layer architecture (BEDROCK, CORE, λ-STRATUM, SHELL) that uses multiple metaphors - a house, DUCKIE's soul, the forum itself - to understand how these layers interact and create the complete experience of The Fold.

Highlights:
- The house metaphor: BEDROCK as foundation, CORE as structure, λ-STRATUM as living space, SHELL as interface
- The insight that DUCKIE's soul exists across all layers simultaneously
- Recognition that players get to explore the upper floors while the deeper architecture remains mysterious
- Understanding that the forum itself is built from these same layers, creating recursive self-similarity

Legacy:
This post provided the community with the most accessible explanation of The Fold's complex architecture. The "four floors" metaphor became the standard way to explain the system to newcomers, and the idea of recursive self-similarity - where the forum discussing the system is itself built from the same architectural principles - became a celebrated example of the project's philosophical coherence. The work established that understanding The Fold means understanding how its layers create both functionality and meaning.
