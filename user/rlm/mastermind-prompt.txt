### Mastermind Play Briefing

**1. Command Syntax**
*   **Guess:** `(eval (mastermind-guess! '(c1 c2 c3 c4)))`
    *   *Note:* `c1`–`c4` must be from: `red`, `blue`, `green`, `yellow`, `white`, `orange`.
*   **Check Status:** `(eval (mastermind-done?))`
*   **Submit Result:** `(submit (mastermind-result))` (Call only when game ends).

**2. Feedback Logic**
*   **`exact`**: Count of colors in the **correct position**.
*   **`misplaced`**: Count of correct colors in **wrong positions**.
*   **Constraint**: No double-counting. A single peg cannot contribute to both `exact` and `misplaced`.

**3. Recording & Memory**
*   Use `(journal 'guesses "G1: (r b g y) -> e=2 m=0")` to record each guess compactly.
*   Use `(journal 'deductions "red confirmed at pos 1")` to record confirmed facts.
*   Use `(recall 'guesses)` or `(recall 'deductions)` to retrieve your records.
*   `(think ...)` is for reasoning — it feeds reflection notes but is not stored in journal.
    Use it when you need to work through complex deductions, not every turn.

**4. Deduction Strategy**
*   **Repetition:** Assume colors **CAN** repeat. Do not assume unique colors.
*   **Ambiguity Handling:** If `exact=N` and `misplaced=0`, you know $N$ positions are correct, but **not which ones**. You must track all $\binom{4}{N}$ possibilities until proven otherwise.
*   **Process:**
    1.  Start with information-gathering guesses.
    2.  After each guess, journal the result and update deductions.
    3.  Use `(recall 'guesses)` to review all feedback before your next guess.
    4.  Select the next guess to maximize information gain or confirm the code.
