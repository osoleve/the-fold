# Hunt the Wumpus: Operational Briefing

### 1. Command Syntax
*   **Move:** `(eval (wumpus-move! 'room-name))`
    *   *Constraint:* Must be an adjacent room. Invalid moves waste a turn.
*   **Shoot:** `(eval (wumpus-shoot! '(room1 room2 ...)))`
    *   *Constraint:* Path of 1–3 adjacent rooms starting from current location. Hits if Wumpus is anywhere on the path.
*   **Look:** `(eval (wumpus-look!))`
    *   *Cost:* 1 move. Re-examines current room.
*   **Check Status:** `(eval (wumpus-done?))`
*   **Submit:** `(submit (wumpus-result))`
    *   *Trigger:* Call immediately upon game end (Win, Eaten, Fell, or Out of Moves).

### 2. Resource Limits
*   **Arrows:** 3 total.
*   **Moves:** 25 total.
*   **Death Conditions:** Entering Wumpus room (Eaten) or Pit room (Fell).

### 3. Sense Interpretation
*   **Stench:** Wumpus is in an **adjacent** room.
*   **Draft:** Pit is in an **adjacent** room.
*   **No Senses:** All adjacent rooms are currently safe (no Wumpus, no Pit).

### 4. Critical Safety Protocol
*   **NEVER** enter an unvisited room if you sense **Stench** or **Draft**.
*   **NEVER** enter a room marked as a suspect.
*   **Action:** Retreat to a known safe (visited) room to re-evaluate. Use arrows to probe suspects; do not walk into them.

### 5. Triangulation Strategy (Finding the Wumpus)
1.  **Identify:** If **Stench** is detected, mark **ALL** currently adjacent rooms as "Wumpus Candidates."
2.  **Retreat:** Move to a previously visited (safe) room.
3.  **Eliminate:**
    *   If the new room has **no Stench**, eliminate any candidate rooms that are *not* adjacent to the new room.
    *   If the new room has **Stench**, update the candidate list to only include rooms adjacent to *both* the previous and current locations.
4.  **Intersect:** Repeat from 2-3 different safe vantage points. The intersection of candidate sets will narrow down to 1 or 2 specific rooms.
5.  **Execute:** Fire an arrow through the final suspect room(s).

### 6. Pit Avoidance Strategy
*   Apply the same logic as Wumpus triangulation.
*   If **Draft** is sensed, mark adjacent rooms as "Pit Candidates."
*   **Rule:** Never enter a candidate room. Retreat to a safe room and approach the suspected pit area from a different direction to confirm or eliminate candidates.

### 7. Tracking
*   Use `(think ...)` to maintain the set of candidate rooms.
*   Use the `(visited ...)` list to identify safe retreat locations.
